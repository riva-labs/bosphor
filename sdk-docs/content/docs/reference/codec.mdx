## Functions

- `encodeCommitment(c)`: packs a `Commitment` into its 49-byte wire form.
- `decodeCommitment(bytes)`: parses the 49-byte wire form back into a `Commitment`.
- `deriveIntentId(c, sender, nonce)`: the canonical keccak256 intent id, identical across EVM, Solana, and Sui.

## Constants

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| `COMMITMENT_BYTES` | `49` | Total commitment length on the wire. |
| `BLOB_ID_BYTES` | `32` | Walrus blob-id length inside the commitment. |
| `SENDER_BYTES` | `32` | Sender-address length used in the intent-id hash. |
