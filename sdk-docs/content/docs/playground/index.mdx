## What the numbers mean

The quote is one amount in the origin chain's native token. It has two parts:

- **Escrow**: Walrus storage, Sui gas, the LayerZero fee for the proof's return
  trip, a price buffer, and the service margin. It is held by the adapter and
  released to the relayer only when the storage proof arrives, or refunded after
  the deadline if it never does.
- **LayerZero forward fee**: paid to LayerZero at submit to carry the intent to Sui.

Both chains show the exact live forward fee. On Solana the relayer simulates the
LayerZero quote for the page, so no LayerZero SDK is loaded in your browser. See
[What the quote contains](/docs/guides/pay-for-storage#what-the-quote-contains) for the full
pricing model.

## Storing from Solana

The Solana store uses Phantom (`window.phantom.solana`) and
`createBosphorSolanaClientFromWallet`: the SDK builds each transaction, Phantom asks
you to approve it, and the SDK sends it to devnet. Turn on Testnet Mode in Phantom
(Settings, Developer Settings) and pick Solana Devnet so the wallet shows your
devnet balance. Get devnet SOL from the [Solana faucet](https://faucet.solana.com).
Besides the quote, a store needs about 0.003 SOL of account rent and the
transaction fee.
