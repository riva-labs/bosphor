---
title: Contracts
description: The on-chain side of Bosphor on each chain, and the one wire format they share.
---

Bosphor runs one contract per chain. The origin adapters take the user's intent
and payment; the Sui side records the intent, verifies the Walrus store, and sends
the proof back. All of them speak the same 49-byte commitment.

Most integrators never call these directly: `@bosphor/sdk` does it for you. Read
on when you call an adapter from your own contract, index its events, or audit it.
